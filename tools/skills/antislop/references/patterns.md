# Full pattern catalog

Deeper reference for `../SKILL.md`. Reach for this on longer or higher-stakes pieces; the SKILL.md
checklist covers most day-to-day writing on its own.

## Vocabulary

**Common AI-vocabulary words.** Additionally, boasts, bolstered, crucial, deep dive, delve,
enduring, enhance, fostering, garner, highlight, interplay, intricate/intricacies, landscape (used
abstractly), meticulous(ly), pivotal, robust, showcase, tapestry (used abstractly), testament,
underscore, vibrant.

**Unnecessary formality.** Utilize, commence, facilitate, optimal, prior to, subsequently, whilst,
amongst, in the event that, due to the fact that, in order to.

**Abstract metaphor nouns that read as technical but have a plainer word.** Substrate (base),
wedge (in) (add), vector (way, method), locus, vantage, nexus, primitive (as a noun), harness (as
metaphor) (use), surface (as in "API surface"), bedrock, scaffolding (as metaphor), modality,
paradigm, gold-plating (more than the job needs), ratchet (as metaphor) (a limit that only
tightens), evacuate (for moving code) (move out), endgame (the last phase), north star, flywheel.

**Exhausted metaphors.** Treasure trove, double-edged sword, tip of the iceberg, cornerstone,
uncharted waters, beacon, crossroads, catalyst, blueprint, symphony, mosaic.

**Fake-depth verbs.** Showcasing, reflecting, underscoring, highlighting, leveraging, unlocking,
unleashing, harnessing, empowering, illuminating, ensuring, fostering, cultivating.

**Empty scale words.** Ecosystem, landscape, platform, solutions, stakeholders, impactful,
holistic, scalable, future-proof.

## Vague authority and attribution

"Experts believe," "industry reports suggest," "some critics argue," "studies show," "research
indicates," "according to recent reports," with no named source, date, number, or link. Name the
source, or delete the claim.

## Press-release and promotional language

"Serves as," "stands as," "boasts a," "features," "is set to," "aims to," "is proud to announce,"
"has emerged as," "represents a significant milestone," "stands as a testament to," "plays a
crucial role," "nestled," "vibrant," "breathtaking," "renowned," "must-visit," "diverse array,"
"commitment to."

## Chatbot and communication artifacts

"I hope this helps!," "let me know if...," "of course!," "certainly!," "absolutely!," "great
question!," "you're absolutely right!," "found the smoking gun!" Cutoff disclaimers ("while
specific details are limited...") should either find the missing source or be removed, not
hedged around.

## Rhetorical over-construction (beyond the core list)

- **"X. Not Y." correction pair.** Denying an objection nobody raised: "This is about safety. Not
  optics."
- **Correlative inflation.** "Not just X, but Y," "not only X, but also Y," "not because X, but
  because Y," when the contrast is decorative rather than doing real work.
- **Formulaic negation.** "No X. No Y. Just Z." when it reads as a slogan rather than a useful
  distinction.
- **Symmetrical bullets.** Bullets with identical length and structure even when the content
  underneath genuinely differs.
- **Stacked parallel clauses.** "A platform that connects..., empowers..., reimagines..." where
  each clause is vague enough to apply to anything.
- **Synonym cycling.** Protagonist, main character, central figure, hero, all in one paragraph.
  Pick one term and repeat it.
- **False ranges.** "From X to Y" where X and Y aren't actually points on a meaningful scale. List
  the topics directly instead.
- **Formulaic challenges.** "Despite challenges, it continues to thrive." Replace with the actual,
  specific facts.

## Structural neatness (a whole piece can be a tell, not just a sentence)

- Every paragraph roughly the same length.
- Every section forced into the same three-part framework.
- Headings that sound interchangeable with each other.
- A conclusion that just restates the introduction in tidier words.
- Every bullet opens with the same bold-label-plus-colon pattern.
- Too many sentences sitting in the same 15-25 word band; vary length on purpose.
- Repeated participial endings across a document: "X does Y, enabling Z," "X creates Y, helping
  Z."

## Voice flatness

No concrete stakes. No visible tradeoff or decision. Perpetual balance ("both sides present valid
points," "reasonable people may disagree") when the piece actually needs a view. Every claim
phrased at the same bland confidence level, regardless of how certain it actually is.

## AI-tool citation artifacts (mechanical check, not a style judgment)

Leftover markup from pasting AI-tool output, not stylistic at all: `contentReference`, `oaicite`,
`turn0search0`, `attributableIndex` (ChatGPT); `[cite: 1]`, `[span_1]` (Gemini); `grok_card`,
`grok_render_citation_card_json` (Grok); lenticular brackets or dagger symbols (DeepSeek);
`attached_file`, `ppl-ai-file-upload` (Perplexity). If any of these strings appear in text meant
for a reader, it's a bug: delete it.
